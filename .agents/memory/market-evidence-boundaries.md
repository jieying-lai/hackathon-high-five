---
name: Market evidence and future scenarios
description: Product trust boundary between observed market signals, older Malaysia data, and hypothetical planning lenses.
---

Separate dated observations from future planning assumptions. A refreshable global remote-job sample must never be presented as live Malaysian hiring data. Posting mentions are not job-growth rates, personal competence, automation probabilities or career safety grades.

**Why:** The user wants an understandable, interactive radar with updatable sources and multiple uncertain futures. The old prototype mixed historical data with hard-coded threat and resilience numbers, making unsupported claims appear measured.

**How to apply:** Keep source scope, sample size and original observation dates visible. Show source failures or retained stale evidence explicitly. Alternative scenarios may change learning emphasis, but never rewrite observed counts or imply statistical confidence or probabilities without evidence. Preserve provider attribution and refresh limits.
