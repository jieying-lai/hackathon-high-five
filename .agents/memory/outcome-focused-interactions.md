---
name: Outcome-focused interactions
description: Product decision about useful engagement, honest progress and separation of personal exploration from hiring evidence.
---

Prioritize user-chosen, reversible actions that lead to actual exploration, practice or application preparation. Keep secondary detail expandable rather than creating more competing summaries.

**Why:** The user asked for a rigorous commercial and usability review, rejecting flat, overly procedural interactions. More animation or more personality labels do not prove useful progress or willingness to pay.

**How to apply:** Treat plans, reflections and completion as private self-reports. Do not turn them into CV facts, certified skills, hiring probabilities or employer-shared data without a separate, appropriate process and consent. Page visits are not achievements. Preserve cancellation, undo, errors and deletion paths. Market positioning and proposed pricing remain hypotheses until supported by real pilot outcomes.
