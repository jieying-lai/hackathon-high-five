Privacy, PDPA & Fair-AI Commitment

Malaysian PDPA 2010 Compliance & Ethical AI Governance

⚖️Malaysian PDPA 2010 Legal Compliance

In strict adherence to the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Act 709) of Malaysia, CareerLens AI collects and processes candidate data solely with explicit consent under the Notice and Choice Principle.

1.
Pseudonymised Unique ID & Purpose Limitation:

Every account is assigned a secure cryptographic identifier. Your data is strictly utilised for skill taxonomy extraction, role matching, candidate screening, and personalised course pathways. Data is never rented, sold, or shared with third-party advertisers.

2.
Industry-Standard Cryptographic Protection:

Passwords are protected using per-user cryptographically random salts and PBKDF2 / SHA-256 key stretching algorithms. Sensitive personal resumes remain in your private vault until you explicitly apply for a vacancy.

3.
Fair-AI & Non-Discrimination Guardrail:

Protected demographic attributes including age, gender, ethnicity, religion, marital status, and disability are strictly stripped from all AI scoring engines and semantic embeddings. Human recruiters retain 100% final decision authority; AI acts strictly as an explainable recommendation assistant.

4.
Data Sovereignty, Inspection & Revocation (Section 30 & 38):

You retain complete ownership of your career records. You may request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your profile and submitted applications at any time by contacting the Admin Helpdesk at admin@careerlens.my with guaranteed processing within 48 business hours.
