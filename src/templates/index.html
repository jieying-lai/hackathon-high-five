    🛡️ Privacy Data Governance, Malaysia PDPA & AI Fairness Terms

    1. Explicit Purpose of Data Collection & Unique ID Assignment

    Every registered user receives a unique Data Governance ID (CL-JS-2026-XXXX or CL-HR-2026-XXXX). Uploaded CVs, skills, and questionnaire responses are processed strictly to compute explainable career match scores, identify skill gaps, and recommend verified reskilling courses.

    2. Password Encryption & Storage Security (Malaysia PDPA 2010)

    Passwords are never stored in plain text. CareerLens AI hashes all passwords using salted PBKDF2-SHA256 before storing in our local SQLite database (careerlens.db). In accordance with Hackathon Domain 1 rules, demo profiles use synthetic or open data only.

    3. Responsible AI, Anti-Bias & Human-in-the-Loop Guarantee

    Protected personal attributes (age, gender, ethnicity, religion, marital status) are strictly excluded from scoring algorithms. Unstated skills on a CV are flagged as "Pending Verification" via follow-up questions rather than automatic rejection. Final career and hiring decisions always remain with humans.

    4. User Control, Retention & Right to Deletion

    Users and employers retain full ownership of their profile data and may export their SDG Career Passport or request immediate deletion/reset of their session data at any time.
